Pingus is a free and open-source Lemmings-style puzzle game featuring cute penguins.

Command groups of marching penguins through intricate obstacle courses by assigning individual skills such as Digging, Bridging, Climbing, Floating, Bashing, and Mining to ensure safe arrival at the exit!

Features of this modernized Android edition:
• Complete classic campaign with dozens of tutorial, holiday, and challenging levels across varied seasons and biomes.
• Touch-optimized gameplay: tap empty areas to smoothly scroll/pan around levels, and tap penguins directly to assign actions.
• Mobile UI Auto-Scaling: seamlessly adapts interface, text, and level elements to modern high-DPI smartphone and tablet screens (with selectable scale in the Options menu).
• Full integrated audio: authentic sound effects and tracker module music powered by OpenAL Soft and libmodplug.
• Modernized toolchain targeting Android 15/16+ (SDK 35) with 16 KB page-size kernel alignment and native 64-bit performance (arm64-v8a and x86_64).
• In-game Level Editor and mod levelsets support.
• 100% free and open-source under GPL-3.0+, with zero ads or tracking.
